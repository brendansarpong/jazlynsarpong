About Me

A little about how I work.

I'm currently a second-year graduate student pursuing my Master's in Public Relations and Corporate Communication at NYU School of Professional Studies. I am showcasing my expertise in strategic messaging, reputation management, and media relations. I thrive at the intersection of trend forecasting and narrative building, always looking for creative ways to connect brands with their key audiences in an ever-evolving media landscape.

Driven by a fascination with how stories shape culture, media, and consumer behavior, I am a strategic communicator with over three years of diverse professional experience across various industries such as education, sales, and product development. This multi-industry background gives me a unique lens for understanding product lifecycles, audience behavior, and the cross-functional dynamics required to execute high-impact campaigns.
